01

Moodboard

What we took from the reference

  • A muted slate ground, calm enough to sit behind a busy diagram for an hour.
  • One flat lavender, used only where your eye should go next.
  • Flat cards with small corners and a soft shadow. No borders fighting each other.
  • def go()
    Monospace for code, and for headings, so the app speaks the same language as the code it maps.

Left out on purpose: the wallpaper, the ASCII art, the layout and the extra pastel swatches. Four colours only.

08

Rules of thumb

  • Lavender means "look here" or "do this next". One per view if you can.
  • No gradients, glows or glass. Shadows stay soft and short.
  • Tell things apart by shape, icon, thickness and dash before colour.
  • No icon on every heading. A word is enough.
  • Write like a patient friend: "Click a box to see its code."
  • No slogans like "Unlock your potential". Say what the button does.
